## High severity

Resolve these contracts before IOS-001 acceptance and LEAD-002 interface freeze. Implementation evidence belongs to the downstream gates.

### H1. Narration events cannot serve as the demonstration state contract

A06 and §§5, 8 describe approximate synchronization with playback events. PD-07 and PD-13 require deterministic timed mathematical actions and continuity on rotation. Replaying a clip after a counter moves can otherwise apply that move twice, narrate the wrong split or leave learner interaction locked.

**Proposed solution.** Use a small typed cue list keyed to versioned local clips and the player's position. Distinguish visual cues from typed math actions. Reconstruct demonstration state from a known baseline and ordered cues through the current position on replay or restoration. Keep a separate learner workspace with an explicit observe-to-try transition and valid starting model. Give cue targets stable semantic IDs, resolve their current geometry in the renderer, and ignore callbacks from superseded playback. Rotation keeps the active clock. Interruption pauses and process recovery restores paused. Reduced motion, muted playback and media failure provide chapter text and a complete step-through demonstration. Media cues never submit attempts.

**Dependencies and evidence.** DATA-001/002 define and validate cue order, duration, targets and allowed actions. EDU-001 and CREATIVE-001 supply transcript, timing, locale, rights and placeholder fixture assets. IOS-004/005 own action and playback adapters. IOS-006/QA-001 exercise U07 through pause, replay, interruption and rotation around cue boundaries, verifying conserved counters, unchanged learner work and zero duplicate scores.

### H2. Session lifetime and recovery are underspecified

Sections 5, 7, 9 and 10 promise checkpointing, but do not specify what owns the session across SwiftUI layout rebuilds or what happens when a pending submission and an ink save overlap. A late recognition result can attach to changed ink or a different item. A crash can strand a pending attempt or restore the wrong drawing revision despite unique attempt IDs.

**Proposed solution.** Root the session above navigation and layout branches. Define checkpoint fields and a recovery transition table for item snapshot and seed, activity and assistance, math model, draft answer, ink revision, media position and pending attempt. Serialize transitions per session. Accept recognition callbacks only for the active item and ink revision. Commit score, evidence and advancement together under one stable submission ID. Write an immutable ink revision before publishing its reference and retain referenced revisions through crash recovery. Specify back navigation, rotation, OS interruption, relaunch and content-version mismatch separately. Scope the beta to one writable lesson session and define ownership if another scene opens.

**Dependencies and evidence.** LEAD-002 reconciles runtime and storage state with DATA-001. IOS-002/003/005 and DATA-003 agree lifecycle and revision boundaries. QA-001 and QA-002 exercise rotation during drawing, recognition, feedback and narration, plus process termination immediately before and after submission commit. Verify one score, intact ink and the correct resumed activity.

### H3. Assistance and assessment need precise evidence transitions

Sections 5–6 record hints and mode, but do not define the scope or ordering of exposures. A worked example replay, diagnostic skip, or retry of an exposed answer could be counted as independent if the badge or presentation phase is used as its proxy. PD-08/14/19 require progressive help, adaptation and equivalent independent evidence for mastery.

**Proposed solution.** Define a deterministic transition table driven by bounded target, item identity, assistance exposure, observed error and prior evidence. Record the assistance actually provided before evaluation. Define neutral retry, representation hint, guided action, reteach and a fresh unexposed independent item. A fresh seed alone is insufficient if it regenerates the exposed question. Keep captions, accessible controls, confirmed transcription and keypad neutral when they convey no answer. Distinguish diagnostic recommendation, parent override and mastery. Preserve mode, target bounds and relevant versions for reporting. Use educator-reviewed representation and misconception mappings with a generic fallback for ambiguous errors.

**Dependencies and evidence.** MATH-000 and Education own semantics and thresholds. DATA-001 represents allowable branches and hint sequences. EDU-001 authors the U07 example and fresh check. MATH-001 tests guided success, repeat exposure, neutral fallback, Lab activity, placement skip and independent retention. Lead freezes only after runtime, content and evidence fixtures agree.

## Medium severity

### M1. Shared math tools need a domain and interaction boundary

Section 3 lists MathViews but leaves the owner of tool state and invariants implicit. Put typed state and legal operations in pure Swift, render them in native views, and inject guided, demonstration and free-exploration rules at the session boundary. Only a lesson assessment handler may produce evidence. Use semantic targets and shared tap-select/place and accessibility actions alongside drag. Begin with counters and number bonds for U07 and add unit-required tools only. Avoid a canvas engine, a generic widget scripting language and one package per feature. DATA-001, Education and IOS-004 should share conservation, zero, reset, bond consistency and accessible interaction fixtures. The Lab can reuse the components when its route is enabled. [Architecture §§3–5](../ARCHITECTURE.md#3-component-model-and-boundaries)

### M2. Expressiveness and tactile audio need explicit ownership

Section 8 mixes character state and narration without defining the interface to a reusable hand. Keep character poses, narration and tactile audio in the presentation layer. Derive speaking from actual playback and thinking, waiting, encouragement and correction from semantic session events. Define a reusable teaching hand with semantic anchor, move, point, contact and release, plus a static highlight and text alternative. Use one simple native prototype with reduced motion and validate before selecting a third-party runtime or authoring lip sync across the curriculum. Keep independent voice and SFX controls and ensure tactile sounds yield to narration. Attentiveness needs no microphone. CREATIVE-001 and IOS-005 depend on owner review of character/hand assets, offline voice samples, rights and budget. QA checks intelligibility, overlays, reduced motion and playback failure. [Architecture §8](../ARCHITECTURE.md#8-juni-and-audio)

### M3. One workbook model must support guided items and sheets

Section 7 covers a single drawing and numeric box. Use stable worksheet, item and answer-region identities, consistent ink coordinates independent of viewport, and separate working from candidate and confirmed answers. Preserve in-session undo/redo across reflow and define history behavior after relaunch. A sheet can reuse the same per-item drawing/answer model and load only visible work; a separate scoring route adds risk. IOS-003 and DATA-003 should specify save and reopen, erased-drawing recovery, stale results, crop coordinates, left/right-handed ergonomics and keypad parity. On a physical iPad measure supported-digit coverage, confusions and latency using synthetic samples and an authorized stylus trial. Keep the release decision tied to the supported unit answer contract and fallback, with complex recognition deferred. [Architecture §7](../ARCHITECTURE.md#7-workbook--recognition)

### M4. Recommendations lack a daily plan and navigation contract

Section 6 returns priority recommendations and §10 mentions a daily goal. PD-18 and the home brief also require a plan the shell can render coherently. Use a local projection over resume state, installed content, review due, eligible targets and parent settings. A projected sequence can expose review, learn, independent practice and an optional challenge with estimated duration. Preserve an active lesson across midnight, permission or plan refresh according to a documented policy. Recompute the rest on relevant changes with an injected clock, and test no plan, unavailable content, review due, interrupted session and unit complete. Avoid a scheduling service, streaks or time-based evidence. IOS-002/008 consume the projection and feature availability for Home, Explore, Math Lab, Workbook and Garden, with protected Parents routed separately. Landscape and portrait share navigation identity. Lead to resolve which plan, worksheet and placement behaviors ship at each gate. [Architecture §§6, 10](../ARCHITECTURE.md#6-math-and-mastery-contracts)

### M5. Parent protection and local storage need operational rules

Sections 9–11 identify encryption, Keychain, rate limiting and deletion, but leave real recovery and backup behavior open. Define data classification, backup/restore policy for database, related files and preferences, Keychain recovery after restore, durable retry throttling, parent access expiry and purge scope (ink, attempts, checkpoints, derived data, caches and app-switcher exposure). Parent reporting reads the same bounded evidence and distinguishes lesson completion, guided work, independent success and retention. Exercise lock/background/restore, crash during deletion and control changes on a synthetic profile. DATA-003 and IOS-007 need a migration fixture and a storage choice based on transactional behavior and maintenance cost. GRDB remains a candidate pending dependency review. Treat child-access gating and device data protection as separate controls. Public distribution and cloud require separate privacy and release decisions. [Architecture §§9–11](../ARCHITECTURE.md#9-storage-and-recovery)

### M6. Device and content scalability require measurable gates

Sections 4 and 12 have useful provisional targets but no workload or target-device evidence. ENV-002 establishes a simulator scaffold with an iPadOS 17 deployment setting and orientation declarations. Physical iPad/Pencil compatibility, minimum-OS runtime and synchronization performance remain unverified. Measure cold lesson load, peak memory, ink/drawing latency, cue drift and recognition turnaround under concurrent playback and rendering. Include rotation, larger text, VoiceOver focus and actions, reduced motion, contrast, captions and end-to-end offline completion. Keep essential audio information available as text/visual steps and keep mathematical operations touch- and VoiceOver-accessible. For P1–P6, exercise representative level/track IDs, core/enrichment separation, prerequisite graphs, historical generator compatibility and bounded exact arithmetic. Index catalog and evidence lookups and load active assets/drawings on demand before adding infrastructure. Set budgets using actual target iPad observations and representative media. ENV-001/002 and Release confirm device/signing readiness, DATA-002/004 validate representative packages, QA-001/002 own device results. Defer signed downloads, sync, a studio and cross-device history until separately scoped. [Architecture §§4, 12](../ARCHITECTURE.md#12-acceptance-and-verification-plan)
