## R2 · Carry accepted CUR-002 semantics into the runtime contract

§4 preserves bounded ranges and §6 correctly requires equivalent independent evidence to waive guided instruction. However, §4 groups unit prerequisites into bounded skills and refers to a “full-range prerequisite graph,” while §6 prioritizes “missing bounded prerequisites before eligible core work.” Together these allow readiness checks or preceding slots to be interpreted as mastery locks. The first-unit draft also says U01–U10 build in order. Its wording cannot settle the distinction.

State the three relationships explicitly. Core slot order is advisory. Readiness probes reflect the current task's quantities and representations, and equivalent evidence or scaffolding can support entry and exploration. Actual component-skill prerequisites govern independent mastery of a dependent target and can be established by prior or concurrent independent evidence. Objective-level links summarize concept relationships and do not require full mastery of every linked objective. For U07, count and match six for entry, then check or support counting in the range needed for the next task, including eight. Comparing and ordering, spelling, handwriting and full-range mastery are unnecessary prerequisites. Keep skip and parent override as recommendation changes with provenance. Require equivalent independent evidence when waiving instruction or crediting mastery.

## Failure-path review

- **Clock and replay.** Given the same baseline, clip version and sampled position, a fold through cues at or before that position in offset/ordinal order yields the same demonstration state after a missed tick, seek or sequential playback. Replaying samples time zero under a new epoch. Rotation rebinds geometry while keeping the clock. Interruption samples and pauses. Relaunch projects the saved position paused. The manual path folds intervening actions through the same boundaries. Explicit Try starts from its own authored baseline. Reopening Observe preserves draft, ink and tool state, and media callbacks cannot submit, grade or advance an assessment.
- **Ownership and recognition.** The coordinator survives view rebuilds. A second scene stays read-only until the previous lease is sealed and its transitions drain successfully. Item identity, revision, answer region and request epoch fence recognition after edits or replacement. Reflow preserves the logical revision. Editing confirmed OCR source ink before submit invalidates that answer. Keypad answers retain separate provenance.
- **Ink and scoring.** The sealed submission captures the answer, working and exact ink references. Immutable bytes precede durable references. Before prepare, restart opens the last durable draft and requires explicit submission. After prepare, restart completes the same frozen submission with the same captured revision. After commit with a lost reply, the transaction returns the original receipt and cursor without another score. Unique submission and occurrence/ordinal keys protect against duplicate attempts. Revision-checked Next protects against a double advance. Cleanup preserves all referenced files. The document explicitly allows loss of edits newer than the last durable checkpoint on OS kill.
- **Assistance.** The reducer records applicable exposure before releasing the visual/audio content and bars submission while the write is unresolved. Restart may retain a disclosure conservatively. Prompt-only replay, accessible controls and confirmed transcription/keypad can preserve eligibility where they supply no measured skill. Solution-revealing feedback or replay registers the applicable question keys without rewriting earlier committed evidence. Replay preserves the chosen help rung. A fresh seed alone supplies no eligibility if it regenerates an exposed equivalent. If eligible fresh items are exhausted, the independent check remains pending.
