## Findings by severity

No critical or high-severity findings.

### Medium

- **M1 · Define active performance for symbol use and algorithms.** `P1-ADD-02-C01/C02` emphasize matching and interpreting, even though the objective constraint calls for using the signs in equations. `P1-ADD-06-C01/C02` say to record place-value work, which could be satisfied by transcription. `P1-TIM-02-C01` could be satisfied by distinguishing labels without selecting and using them in context. Add observable evidence that learners write or complete meaningful equations, independently execute addition and subtraction algorithms, and select or use am/pm in familiar contexts. If exact noon and midnight appear, use explicit labels or a documented convention and avoid ambiguous 12 o'clock prompts.
- **M2 · Clarify how the two grid formats count toward core mastery.** `P1-GEO-04` correctly summarizes the source as dot or square grids, but its constraint requires both and its concept graph requires dot-grid copying before square-grid copying. The coverage report says all constituent concepts are required for objective mastery. Treat the formats as alternative evidence or identify any requirement for both as an explicit product extension for Lead review.
- **M3 · Distinguish readiness from full-range mastery in the graph.** `P1-ADD-01-C01` depends on `P1-NUM-01-C02` (counting larger sets through 100) before small-quantity bonds. The same broad dependency appears before concrete matching, early pairing of sets, ordinals and ruler work. `P1-MON-01-C01` also requires regrouping within 100 before any cent counting. `P1-TIM-01-C01` requires both number-pattern concepts before work with clock hands, although counting by fives is relevant to the later reading skill. Pairing objects, splitting small sets and working with the two clock hands are appropriate early experiences. The first-unit note already describes bounded evidence. At CUR-002 level, clarify which links denote suggested sequence or bounded readiness and which are genuine mastery prerequisites.

### Low

- **L1 · Synchronize the coverage row for P1-L046.** It repeats the noon and midnight clause and diverges from the JSON skill. Align the text when resolving M1.
- **L2 · Tighten the summaries.** For `P1-TIM-03`, include active use of h and min. For `P1-GEO-03`, emphasize identifying component shapes. For `P1-LEN-04-C01`, describe reading to the nearest centimetre with a ruler so the learning target does not imply a separate formal rounding lesson. Introduce “half circle” alongside “semicircle” for continuity with the source and younger learners.
- **L3 · Reconcile first-unit approval metadata through the Lead.** The JSON and coverage treat scope as proposed, while the ledger's historical owner-approval entry records an approved scope pending Education review and upstream acceptance. The Lead should confirm the authoritative status and correct stale metadata. This review makes no unit-scope decision.
