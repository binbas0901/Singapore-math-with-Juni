## Contract defects and requested corrections

1. **R1 · Stopping rules disagree at four same-date successes when the contingency is absent.** The planned-assessment section makes the fifth slot optional, then directs the app to keep a group open and reserve a later opportunity after four same-date successes, and also directs it to close when the planned opportunities are consumed. A pinned four-slot plan in this state has no later slot to reserve. Specify that a later successful date must be feasible under the pinned plan before offering the last relevant slot, and explicitly state the disposition for an already exhausted plan. An eligible reserved fifth permits waiting for later evidence. A temporarily unavailable candidate within a feasible plan stays pending. A fully consumed plan without a second successful date cannot pass. Keep the consent decision on exact stopping and replacement mechanics separate from fixing the inconsistent instructions. [Mastery contract, planned assessment](../mastery-contract.md#planned-initial-assessment-f2)
2. **R2 · Parent presentation lacks an enforceable acceptance example.** The contract specifies bounded labels and separate evidence counts, but does not state how they must appear together when all four successes are familiar. A headline can imply transfer or later retention even if the detail screen has accurate counts. Require the visible achievement summary to identify the demonstrated scope, familiarity, transfer observation and retention status, with an explicit all-familiar acceptance case. The educational accuracy requirement follows the existing owner decision; final wording and UI remain open.
