### L1 clarification for Lead disposition

State explicitly that the U01–U10 order is advisory and that U07 counting and numeral matching are task-bounded readiness probes. When readiness remains unresolved, the recommended guided route can continue practice and defer its scheduled independent check. Exploration remains available as allowed by parent settings, and the readiness probe creates no additional eligibility condition for otherwise valid independent evidence. A parent choice or diagnostic recommendation may change the starting point. Equivalent independent evidence is required to treat guided instruction for that bounded target as already demonstrated. Only a documented component-skill mastery prerequisite can affect mastery eligibility, with prior or concurrent independent evidence. U07 has no such prerequisite in the accepted L016 mapping. Comparing, ordering, spelling, handwriting and full-range mastery remain outside U07 readiness.
