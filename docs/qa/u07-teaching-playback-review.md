## Material defect

**Manual counting shows the completed count without demonstrating how to count the objects one at a time.** Start the lesson and tap **Next step** once. All six overlays appear at once, with equal visual emphasis. In landscape they run across one row. In portrait the learner must move from **Four** at the far right of the first row to **Five** at the left of the second row. The same behavior recurs in the four-counter tray count and the recombined six-counter count. XCTest confirms the six initial labels are distinct and none of the counter elements is selected in the manual count.

The overlays correctly label each object in reading order and work as a completed reference. For a P1 learner who needs to see how each successive word is assigned to exactly one object, the manual mode depends on already reading and following ordinal order. The draft calls for ordered static indications as well as count words. Give the learner a nonanimated way to follow the count one counter at a time, with a visible indication and an equivalent accessible cue. Verify the opening count, the remaining tray count and the recount, including the portrait row wrap.

## Nonblocking improvements

- The hand symbol is next to the tray or bowl heading. The actual target is identified by its outline and, during counting, its count word. Removing the distant hand would reduce the number of places competing for attention.
- The remaining counters reflow during each transfer, briefly overlapping the moving counter in portrait. Holding their positions during the transfer would make the moved object easier to follow. The settled states and counter identities are correct.
- The Observe panel is titled **Number bond** when only **Whole 6** is shown at 12 seconds. Introduce the title with the full diagram to align the label with the point where Juni names it.
