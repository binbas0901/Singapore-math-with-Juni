## Curriculum and assessment trace

All stated numerical answers in U01–U12, the U11 matrix and both U07 examples are correct. The progression from cardinality and representation to comparison, ordering and part–whole reasoning is appropriate for introductory P1. Keep zero as the size of an empty set. When learners meet 10 on a ten-frame or number track, teach it as a quantity and numeral at this stage. The unit draws on six p. 31 objectives within 0–10. Formal place value, operation symbols, inverse equations, ordinals and patterns can remain in their later slots.

## Findings and acceptance conditions

**M1 · U04 / U11 — define the number-word writing boundary.** If U04 claims writing number words, teach how to produce one and include a fresh independent check, with evidence for word production distinguished from quantity and numeral understanding. If the intended slice is numeral reading/writing and number-word recognition, label the L007 coverage explicitly as recognition and identify writing words as later coverage. Provide access supports that preserve the measured skill. For example, reading a printed number word aloud to the learner can support mathematics access, but that response cannot establish independent reading of the word. Confirm the intended bounded target with Curriculum and escalate any change in learning outcomes under the owner decision rules.

**M2 · U11 — assess the skills taught within each objective.** Add a fresh making-ten item with a different known part from U10 and capture a small sample of independent modelling or explanation for part–whole operations. A learner can build, draw or point to the whole and both parts, join groups and identify the result, or show what was removed and what remains. Preserve the U07–U10 lesson checks and use the U11 evidence map to name which checks contribute to the unit record. A single ADD-01 score would hide the differences among splitting, joining, removing and making ten. Include equivalent fresh evidence on a later day and record range, representation and help used. Leave thresholds and review intervals to MATH-000.

**M3 · U07 — connect representations and protect the independent check.** In the guided example, count six once, move two into one group and move the four remaining counters into the other group, or explicitly label their starting area as the second group. Keep each counter in exactly one place and physically recombine the groups so the learner can verify that the whole is still six. Align the whole and part labels with the counters before showing the diagram alone. Let the learner fill a missing numeral on a fresh guided example after seeing the complete 6–2–4 bond. In the independent 8-with-3 check, show the whole and known part without marking, highlighting or pre-counting the missing five. Allow learner-selected counters or drawing and ask the learner to show where five belongs and how the parts make eight. Start the independent item from a fresh state. Make replay of directions and answer entry alternatives available. If a task-specific cue, guided move or revealed bond is used, record the assisted response and offer a fresh equivalent independent item. Review this observe–try–explain sequence before the slice is authored.

**L1 · Prerequisites — make readiness and recovery explicit.** U01 and U02 build one-to-one counting and cardinality. U03 should introduce the numeral labels it asks learners to match, or briefly check familiarity and teach any needed labels before practice. U04 builds from those matches. U05 paired comparison starts from one-to-one matching, and U06 ordering builds from bounded numeral comparison. For U07, count and match six as a quick entry probe, then check or support counting within the range needed for the next task, including eight. Route a learner who needs support to a brief counting or numeral-matching activity and recheck before independent evidence. Comparing and ordering, spelling, handwriting and full-range mastery are unnecessary prerequisites for U07. Keep the recommended sequence and allow placement skips with equivalent independent evidence for the particular bounded target.

**L2 · Pacing — reconcile the draft duration and plan stopping points.** Apply PD-06’s flexible 10–15 minutes and update the draft’s earlier 8–12 minute reference. A plausible U07 allocation is about 2 minutes for readiness, 3 for narration and demonstration, 4 for manipulation and diagram practice, and 3 for independent work and explanation. Resume at meaningful activity boundaries or shorten redundant practice when understanding is clear. U11 has more response opportunities than a typical guided lesson. Present short groups of items and permit a break and resume while preserving which evidence was independent. The estimates are planning assumptions. Elapsed time does not determine mastery.

## Narration, demonstration and assistance

- Use short local ElevenLabs clips to direct attention to the whole, the action and its mathematical meaning. Speak the prompt, move the counters at the relevant cue and leave quiet time for the child to act and explain. Verify the transcript, number names, intelligibility and timing with representative learners when recordings exist. The compact header, replay, captions and mute support access when the mathematical model and instructions remain complete without sound.
- Use the virtual hand to model one meaningful move during the worked example. Keep its gesture from covering objects or labels and provide a static or reduced-motion equivalent with the same mathematical information. Separate observe and try states. Replay the demonstration from its deliberate starting state without moving the learner’s counters or changing assessment evidence. Provide a touch alternative to dragging and a keypad or accessible response option. Confirm uncertain recognition before grading.
- Apply PD-08 to observed work. For a response of six in the guided example, a neutral retry can ask which part the question is asking about. A later hint can identify the group to count, then guided interaction can show how to move or count each counter once. If needed, reteach with a different split before returning to an equivalent independent item. Check the learner’s action or explanation before inferring a misconception. Generic directions and access supports can stay available during assessment when they leave the mathematical work to the learner.
- In U07, manipulation, a connected bond diagram and a missing numeral supply the concrete, pictorial and abstract experiences. Use the same connection where it serves the other sessions, such as pairing objects, drawing matches and then comparing quantities. Preserve the learner’s ability to move back to an appropriate representation and explain how it relates to the answer.
