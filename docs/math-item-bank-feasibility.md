# MATH-000 · ADD-01 item-bank feasibility

**Finding.** A strict initial make-ten check has no guaranteed four-success path after normal instruction and cross-task exposure. A worked U10 example using 4 + 6 consumes one of six unordered relationships. If U11 removal also discloses 2 + 8 before make-ten, four remain including zero and three remain with positive parts only. Four remaining allow four perfect responses across two successful learning dates, subject to facet coverage. They cannot absorb one scored miss. A third exclusion makes four new successes impossible. Keep the four-success requirement and report the evidence actually available while the owner decides how familiar retrieval may be used outside retention.

This is a synthetic authoring and policy proposal under D02, D03, D07–D10 and D13. CUR-003 supplies task specifications. The extra quantities, slot allocations, facet definitions, mappings and timing proposals below require Education review and, where they change assessment policy, owner approval. ## Reversible operational trial proposals · approval pending

- **Replacement and stopping.** Authorize at most one preplanned replacement offer per unresolved slot, with its own delivery and an equivalent uncued workspace. Recheck eligibility at offer and submit. A scored miss consumes the slot. An unresolved fallback permits pause or practice. Pin a fifth-slot selection rule before offering any item and reserve later-date and facet coverage. Do not repeatedly offer alternatives while waiting for a success.
- **Mixed retention.** Consider later targeted corroboration qualifying a mixed stage only when the same pinned facet rubric applies, the original complementary success is still within a proposed seven-calendar-day span from pair completion, and the follow-up is a complete eligible independent first response on a later learning date. Apply the elapsed 48-hour gate separately where relevant and check echo even for a different relationship. Anchor the next interval to the follow-up date and record the original miss. A stale, cued or incomplete response cannot qualify. If comparability expires, open a new eligible pair. Until approved, corroboration leaves the stage open.
- **Pause and date attribution.** Trial an initial-group span of 14 calendar days from its first complete scored result and a seven-calendar-day span for completing a started retention pair from its first complete result. Explicitly supersede after expiry or a material rubric/content change, preserving all receipts and exposure. An individually locked numeral can combine with a later model within the pinned span only if the independent demand and applicable exposure still hold. Assign the one success to the verified learning date of completing the required model or numeral, whichever is last. A Monday locked numeral and Tuesday model supply one Tuesday receipt. Wednesday recovery of that receipt keeps Tuesday. Pin profile time zone, separate calendar due dates from elapsed spacing, and defer under clock uncertainty.
- **Repeated difficulty.** End that scored sequence after two complete misses in a pair or a failed single targeted follow-up. Retain the last earned level and evidenced interval. Offer short facet-specific guided practice and later one newly planned eligible pair for the same still-due stage, on a subsequent learning date and after applicable rest. Where repeated practice keeps resetting the embargo or the pool is empty, report pending and allow a pause. Avoid a scored loop within the sitting.
- **Model observation.** Require observable learner construction or structured selection/marking with disjoint and exhaustive groups, correct roles and the required relation; preserve action evidence for any recombination claim. Equivalent touch and accessibility routes must preserve choices. Numeral-only evidence remains separate. A confirmed correct numeral with a complete contradictory model is a miss for the combined outcome. Unverified drawings or ambiguous marking leave the model pending. Any human rubric-based route needs approval of observer authority, accessibility equivalence and local provenance.
- **Post-21-day cadence and burden.** Keep the last dated stage and trial one optional complementary maintenance pair after a further 28 calendar days, with no stacked catch-up, then collect burden and evidence before setting recurring cadence. Initial ADD-01 alone needs 16 successful modeled responses across four targets and can use up to 20 scored slots. Scheduled retention adds 24 responses if all four targets complete the three pairs, before any follow-up. Use short groups within the unit's 10–15 minute pacing, avoid mandatory diagnostics across every target in one sitting and sample actual time, assistance, access friction and unfinished work before committing to ongoing cadence.
