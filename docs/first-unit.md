# CUR-003 — First learning unit: Numbers and Number Bonds to 10

Version 1.2 | Owner scope approved 2026-11-17 | Lead accepted specification following independent Education re-review; owner sample-slice approval pending

## Scope, sequence and pacing

The unit has 11 core sessions and one optional enrichment session. It introduces bounded content from six objectives on printed p. 31 of the verified MOE syllabus. Zero represents the size of an empty set. Introduce 10 as a quantity and numeral. Place value to 100, ordinals, patterns, formal operation notation, inverse equations and comparison subtraction are later content. Number bonds are the unit's instructional model for part–whole reasoning. [Source register](../references/source-register.md) · [P1 coverage map](coverage-p1.md)

Plan for 10–15 minutes per session under PD-06. Shorten redundant practice when the learner shows understanding, extend for support or pause at an activity or worksheet group boundary. Resume from saved progress, keeping item, working and assistance status. U11 can span sittings. Elapsed time is a pacing observation with no effect on grading or mastery.

There is no formal mathematics entry requirement (MOE p. 4). U01–U02 build one-to-one counting and cardinality within the ranges shown. U03 introduces or refreshes the numeral labels needed for each matching task. U04 builds on these matches. U05 starts with one-to-one pairing, and U06 moves from comparing two familiar numerals to ordering several. U07 has its own bounded readiness route. U01–U10 form an advisory sequence, not a mastery gate. Placement or parent choice can change the recommended starting point. Equivalent independent evidence is required to treat guided instruction for that bounded target as already demonstrated. Exploration remains available within parent permissions. Only documented component-skill mastery prerequisites can affect independent mastery eligibility, and prior or concurrent independent evidence can establish them. U11 gathers independent evidence across all six objectives, and U12 is optional after U07–U10.

### Number words in U04 and U11

Teach the printed and spoken forms from **zero** through **ten** within the existing U04 session. Use a reusable word strip and short practice samples rather than requiring all eleven spellings in one sitting. In guided practice, Juni can say the word, show it beside its numeral and model arranging letters in order. The learner can copy or assemble the word with a model, then try building it after the model is hidden. Record copying, preselected letter banks and spelling cues as supported literacy practice.

For independent production, give the spoken target with the direction “Make the word you heard” and keep the target spelling out of the displayed directions. Provide a full alphabet with no word suggestions or letter cues, or a blank handwriting space. The full alphabet provides a way to produce the word without requiring pencil control. Use a separate prompt for printed-word recognition and keep the target word's audio pronunciation out of that check. If an access route supplies the pronunciation of the target, record that support and use a separate mathematics item to assess the associated quantity or numeral. Confirm uncertain numerical recognition before grading. Assess word production from the ordered letters selected or clearly written, without assuming automatic word recognition. Handwriting legibility can be observed separately. Record numeral writing, number-word recognition, word production and mathematical evidence separately. A spelling or letter-formation difficulty does not lower the result of an independently demonstrated count, numeral–quantity match or part–whole model.

## U07 vertical slice

The learner splits a counted whole into two disjoint parts, connects the counters to a bond diagram, and shows that the parts can be recombined into the same whole. Use one focused workspace for each state.

1. **Readiness (about 2 minutes).** Ask the learner to count six counters and match numeral 6. Check counting and numeral matching as needed for the upcoming quantities, including 7 and 8, in a separate counting workspace without previewing any split. If support is needed, revisit a brief U01–U03 activity for the relevant range and recheck with a fresh arrangement before the independent split. Comparison, ordering, spelling, handwriting and full-range mastery are unnecessary for entry. If these task-bounded readiness probes remain unresolved, the recommended guided route may continue practice and defer its scheduled independent check. The probes create no additional eligibility condition for otherwise valid independent evidence, and permitted exploration remains available. U07 has no component-skill mastery prerequisite in the accepted L016 mapping.
2. **Observe (about 3 minutes).** Juni counts six counters in a tray once. At the narrated cues the teaching hand moves two counters, one at a time, into a bowl. Label the tray as the second group and count its four counters. Link the whole label 6 to all the counters and the part labels 2 and 4 to their respective groups. Recombine the counters in the tray and verify that the whole still has six. Show the complete bond with whole 6 and parts 2 and 4 after linking it to the objects. Each counter belongs to one physical location at every step. Narration replay starts from its own baseline. Captions and manual steps convey the same mathematics, with a static or reduced-motion equivalent and a hand gesture that leaves the counters and labels visible.
3. **Try together (about 4 minutes).** An explicit Try action opens a separate learner workspace with seven counters in the tray. Ask the learner to move one into a bowl and identify the tray as the other group. Link both groups to the diagram and ask for the missing numeral 6 in the bond for whole 7 and known part 1. Offer drag and tap-select/tap-place. The learner can return to counters and recombine the parts to explain the result. If reopened, the demonstration preserves the learner's workspace and any relevant exposure follows the assistance rules below.
4. **Show and explain independently (about 3 minutes).** Open a fresh item with whole 8 and known part 3, and leave the other part blank. Supply no partition, highlighted region or pre-counted group for the missing part. Let the learner choose counters or drawing, enter 5 and show where 5 belongs and how the two parts make 8. Replay the prompt, offer equivalent accessible interactions and provide keypad or confirmed supported handwriting for numeral entry. Assess the confirmed submission and model. Optional further splits belong in practice or enrichment after the check.

These allocations are flexible planning estimates. Offer stopping points after the demonstration and guided activity, and restore item progress and assistance on resume. Keep Juni and narration controls in a compact header. Use local prerecorded ElevenLabs narration with transcript, replay, mute and manual progression. Pause between narrated actions to leave room to think. Apply moon-only branding.

### Progressive assistance and eligibility

Use observed action or explanation to check whether a learner who answers with the whole has confused the whole with a part, lost track of a counter or simply misread the prompt. Start with a neutral retry that repeats the task without new mathematical information. If needed, offer a visual hint identifying the group to inspect, guide a move or one-to-one count, then reteach with a different split. Choose specific help only when the learner's work supports it. Apply the same progression in other sessions with appropriate representations.

Record applicable exposure before showing or playing a task-specific hint, guided action, worked example or solution feedback, including through replay or captions. Such help makes subsequent work on that question or an exposed equivalent assisted. Preserve the evidence of any submission committed before feedback. Demonstration actions and replay themselves produce zero attempts. After help, offer a fresh unexposed item of comparable demand for that skill. A new seed or layout alone does not establish freshness. If eligible items are exhausted, finish as practice with independent evidence pending. Prompt replay and accessible input preserve eligibility when they supply no part of the skill being assessed. Confirm uncertain transcription or allow another input method before grading. Uncertainty and invalid input create no scored evidence or assistance escalation.
