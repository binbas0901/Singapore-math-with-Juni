# CUR-003 — First learning unit: Numbers and Number Bonds to 10
Version 1.0 proposal | 2026-11-17 | Owner scope approved 2026-11-17; Education approval and CUR-002 acceptance pending

## Scope and sequence
11 core sessions plus one optional enrichment session; suggested 8–12 minutes each, flexible. No formal entry requirement (MOE p4); diagnostic matching and counting only. This is an introductory slice of six numbered objectives on p31, not complete P1 coverage. Number bonds are our original instructional strategy. Zero is included as an instructional choice. Place value to 100, ordinals, patterns, formal operation notation and inverse equations remain later content. U01–U10 build in order, U11 requires independent attempts across all targets, U12 optional after U07–U10; placement can skip instruction only after equivalent evidence. Local unit prerequisites are bounded by range, not full-range concept completion.

## Assessment matrix
All examples above are original illustrative items, not a finished bank. For U11 use new examples: count 0 and 7 objects (0,7); match 9 dots to numeral (9); read six and write 4 from audio (6,4); compare sets 2,5,4 (5 largest); order 9,1,6 descending (9,6,1) and compare 3 vs 8 (8 larger); split 8 with known part 3 (5), join 2 and 7 (9), remove 4 from 10 (6). Record evidence by target, independent/assisted status and range 0–10. Recheck with new items on a later day; no full objective mastery or fixed threshold until MATH-000 approval. Reading/writing accommodations: tap or audio alternatives distinguish math evidence from literacy/handwriting evidence.

## Vertical slice recommendation: U07
Readiness: count 6 counters and match numeral 6. Engagement: Juni narrates “Here are six counters. Move two into this bowl. How many are in the other bowl?” child drags, then sees diagram with whole 6 and parts 2 and 4. Abstract: write 4 in missing part, no formal equation required. Mastery: new whole 8, known part 3, answer 5, then explain by moving or drawing; record hint use. Misconceptions: recount whole instead of part, lose a counter, mistake part for whole. Feedback: highlight the two groups and invite recount; never reveal answer before independent submission. Recognition uncertainty prompts confirmation or keypad without penalizing mathematics. Optional challenge find another split of 8. Use moon-only branding and offline prerecorded audio; replay, mute and captions.
