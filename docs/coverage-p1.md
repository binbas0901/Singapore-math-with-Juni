## Recommended core sequence

The numbered core slots give an advisory order. Consult the readiness column for evidence bounded by the quantities or representations used in a particular task. Equivalent evidence is sufficient and scaffolding can support exploration. The mastery prerequisite column lists component skills required for independent mastery of the dependent target. Prior or concurrent independent evidence can establish these skills. The objective-level column summarizes cross-objective links and does not require mastery of all concepts in the referenced objective.

Each slot plans readiness, appropriate concrete/pictorial/abstract engagement, independent evidence and later retrieval. Assessment descriptions for active performance include writing equations, executing written algorithms and using am/pm in context. Exact noon and midnight use explicit labels. Measure segments directly against a ruler to the nearest centimetre.

## Gaps and review requirements

- MOE p. 4 specifies no formal mathematics prerequisite for entry to P1. The sequence and readiness checks above are product planning decisions.
- P1 multiplication uses ×. Division concepts use equal sharing and grouping within the source bound, with division notation deferred to P2. Money uses Singapore currency by default. US localization requires separate review.
- Length activities need a calibrated on-screen ruler or a physical ruler. Geometry grids and clocks need device tests.
- Use original materials for problem solving, reasoning, reflection and feedback (pp. 13–14, 19–20, 24–28). Commercial rights review and independent review of authored activities remain release requirements.
