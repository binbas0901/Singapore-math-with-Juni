## Exact grading and bounded targets

A target identifies the concept, range, task demands and any required representations or facets. Evidence for a split within 0–10 contributes to the bounded split target only. Track splitting, joining, removing and making ten separately within ADD-01. Report both matching directions, comparisons including equality, ordering directions and the distinct U04 outcomes against their authored criteria. Lesson completion and the unit report do not imply mastery of the source objective's full range, P1, or enrichment.

- Grade typed, explicitly confirmed submissions against a validated immutable item. U07 uses exact integers. Validate the whole and parts within the authored domain and require the missing part to be exactly the whole minus the given part. Include empty sets or zero parts where authored. Reject ambiguous, contradictory or out-of-domain content before use. Use checked arithmetic and normalized exact rationals if later tasks require them. Reject a zero denominator.
- Separate answer correctness and required model sufficiency. In U07, 5 is numerically correct for whole 8 and known part 3. A sufficient model identifies all 8 as the whole and two disjoint exhaustive groups of 3 and 5. Counters must each occur once and the roles and counts must agree with the answer. Swapped display positions are equivalent when the roles still make sense. The removed and remaining groups in U09 carry distinct meanings. Accept verified counter states and equivalent drawing or accessible marking under the same rubric. A bare numeral or unverified drawing leaves model evidence pending. Offer a neutral way to indicate the groups before grading a complete response. Keep a confirmed contradictory model as a mathematical error. Free-form working remains available for review.
- Grade the confirmed mathematical value independently of the input method. An uncertain, stale, cancelled or unusable recognition result creates no mathematical grade, progress penalty, or assistance escalation. Keep the work, allow correction and confirmation, or offer keypad entry. An incorrect confirmed answer can count as an error. A corrected transcription before submission is still the first attempt. A valid numerical response can be wrong even when it lies outside the expected answer range. Ambiguous input instead needs clarification.
- Capture numeral recognition, numeral writing, printed number-word recognition and word production as distinct outcomes. For word production accept the ordered letters from an uncued full alphabet or clearly observed writing. Keep formation and legibility observations separate. Reading directions or replaying the same counting prompt can be neutral. Pronouncing the target printed word supplies part of that recognition task. A prefilled spelling or prompted letters are support. Use a separate mathematics prompt to establish quantity or part–whole understanding when the literacy response is uncertain or incorrect.

Resume or recover with that snapshot and a single receipt per submission. Park incompatible pending work. Preserve historical outcomes under their recorded policy and require reviewed equivalence before transferring evidence across target or rubric changes.

Compare candidates on two axes.

| Axis | Proposed rule |
| --- | --- |
| Equivalent demand | Use the same bounded target, task direction, required model and comparable counting burden and scaffolding. Use reviewed coverage slots for difficulty, range and representations. Switching model input can preserve demand if the same relationships must be demonstrated. |

## Evidence and assistance

Record requested mode and actual support separately from correctness and purpose. A readiness probe or diagnostic yields separate observations. A guided success shows performance within planned instruction. An originally independent item becomes assisted for future submissions after applicable help. An eligible independent submission measures unaided work and can be correct or incorrect. Retention is a due independent check with a review purpose. Help during review contributes to practice and remediation. Demo, replay, Lab exploration, session completion, skips and time passing create zero mastery evidence.

| Trigger | Policy and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed error on a fresh independent item | Record the first scored outcome once before feedback. Offer one neutral retry with the same prompt and generic acknowledgment, without highlighting an answer-bearing feature. A correct retry without disclosure remains unaided retry evidence and does not replace the original outcome or fill an independent mastery or retention slot. Active recovery here is a chance to self-correct. A skipped, cancelled or technically incomplete response does not use this opportunity. |
| Further confirmed difficulty or explicit request for help | Offer the first applicable authored scaffold in order, from a visual representation hint to a guided action to reteaching. A learner may opt into teaching or pause at any step. Persist the chosen rung and route on error and observable work only. For example, an answer equal to the whole alone does not establish a misconception. If no reviewed mapping matches, use generic help. |
| Hint or guided action | Point to the relevant group, then guide a move or a count as needed. Seal the exposure before delivery and grade only confirmed learner work. Planned guided practice stays guided, with its scaffold provenance recorded. Subsequent work on a disclosed question outside planned guidance is assisted. |
| Reteach | Use a different authored relationship to explain the same concept. Give short, actionable feedback. Any return to the exposed question stays practice. Record all solution-bearing relations and preserve previous receipts. |
| After assistance | Offer a comparable fresh item from a reserved pool after assisted success or reteaching. Require a new confirmed answer and any required model. Exclude exposed or previously scored keys. Prefer a different answer after displaying the earlier answer, and defer when no reviewed substitute avoids simply echoing a just-revealed value. If no eligible item remains, finish practice and leave the check pending. |

## Order, readiness and placement

Advisory order recommends U01–U10 and U11 consolidation. U12 is optional enrichment. Readiness probes ask whether the learner can handle the specific quantities and representations for an upcoming task. U07 probes counting and matching at 6, 7 and 8 with separate, unsplit sets. When evidence for a needed quantity is unavailable, sample it and offer a short U01–U03 refresher followed by a fresh arrangement if needed. Unresolved readiness can defer a scheduled guided-route check. It does not invalidate an eligible independent result or restrict permitted exploration. Only a documented component-skill mastery dependency affects the dependent mastery label. Prior or concurrent independent evidence can satisfy it within the specified bounds. U07 has no such prerequisite.

Proposed placement uses short optional probe groups with pauses. For each target considered for accelerated entry, offer two fresh, unaided opportunities in different required facets when available, sampling the upper end of the intended range where appropriate. Recommend starting at the earliest sampled target with a confirmed error, then suggest the appropriate refresher or lesson. With two correct and sufficient responses, suggest trying the next target or an independent challenge. Where samples are missing, mixed across required facets, assisted, or interrupted, leave that coverage unresolved and offer instruction or another check. Leave unsampled targets unresolved. Two samples limit the burden of placement and justify only a recommendation. Diagnosis, parent-selected starting point, bypassing a route and placement reset create no mastery. Under this proposal, diagnostic records never convert to mastery evidence. A previously met bounded independent standard can waive corresponding guided instruction. A parent override can change the recommendation or allowed access and is labeled as a local manual choice in reporting.

## Proposed thresholds and retention

Use prospectively selected short check groups per bounded target. Author a coverage plan listing essential facets, their allowable substitutions and comparable variants before creating the item bank. Ensure the initial four successes collectively cover all essential facets, including contrasting structures or ranges when the target requires them. Partition broader targets into smaller evidence groups if four successes cannot cover the rubric. The U07 slice supplies a single first check and a reserved substitute, so it can demonstrate independent eligibility without establishing mastery.

| State | Proposed rule and rationale |
| --- | --- |
| New, emerging, assisted | New has no submitted learning evidence. Emerging has active evidence without supported success or sufficient independent evidence. A successful guided or assisted response can establish the assisted stage. Keep independent successes visible while the learner is still accumulating the threshold. Use text labels alongside architecture moon phases. |
| Independent mastery | Use at most the last five eligible first scored opportunities for that target under the applicable policy. Require at least four correct with sufficient models where required, distinct keys, essential facet coverage, evidence on at least two local learning dates and satisfaction of any genuine component prerequisite. An isolated mistake can coexist with a demonstrated pattern. Different dates and varied items reduce dependence on immediate imitation. U11 may contribute; it may span short sittings. |
| Retention and review | Schedule an initial check 2 days after independent mastery, then 7 days after passing that review, then 21 days after the next pass. Propose a 21-day interval after later passes during beta. At each stage use two fresh, comparable independent items with complementary coverage, requiring two correct first responses with any required models. Rotate coverage across reviews. First pass earns retained. Spacing tests retrieval after an increasing delay while two short items limit fatigue. |
| Review due or needs refresh | A missed date leaves the prior achievement and shows review due. A confirmed review error closes the review as needing refresh, preserves prior history, and recommends targeted practice. After practice, schedule a fresh two-item review on the next learning day at least one calendar day later. A pass returns to retained and restarts the intervals at 7 days. If independent assessment before mastery falls short, offer instruction and a later fresh check. Insufficient items, technical interruptions or access uncertainty leave assessment pending. |

Use an injected clock, fixed profile calendar/time-zone semantics and recorded evaluation dates. Never manufacture learning days through clock rollback or recalculate a committed receipt on restart. Stall future scheduling on ambiguous clock changes until dates can be resolved locally. The beta needs reviewed item pools and synthetic boundary cases before activating these cutoffs. These counts and intervals are starting hypotheses, with no statistical or classroom validation asserted.
