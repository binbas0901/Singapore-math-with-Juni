## Bounded evidence and exact grading (F4, F7)

A target specifies the concept, range, task direction, required facets and representations. Track split, join, removal and make-ten separately within ADD-01. Track both matching directions, comparison including equality, ordering directions, and U04 numeral recognition, formation, printed number-word recognition and word production separately. Label achievements at the demonstrated scope. Word production requires uncued letters from a full alphabet or clearly observed writing. Pronouncing the target printed word supplies evidence the learner was meant to provide. When access changes the literacy task, offer a separate quantity or bond prompt.

- Grade exact confirmed integers against the pinned item and answer set. For U07 require a validated whole and parts in the authored range, and compute the complement exactly. Keep item-domain limits separate from the response grammar. In the static nonnegative-integer fixture, a representable confirmed 11 is an incorrect answer to 8 with known part 3. A negative, fractional, unrepresentable or ambiguous input is outside the supported grammar and invites neutral correction. Keypad, handwriting and serialization must use the same agreed grammar and representable bounds. Uncertain, stale or cancelled recognition yields no mathematical attempt. The learner may confirm a corrected transcription or enter a keypad value before first submit with the same eligibility.
- Record numeral correctness, model outcome, support and purpose independently. A sufficient U07 model establishes the whole and two disjoint exhaustive groups, using each counter once, explicitly mapping known and missing roles and agreeing with the confirmed answer. When zero is assessed, the empty group must be intentional and identifiable. Swapping display positions preserves meaning if roles stay correct. In U09 the removed and remaining roles are distinct. A correct numeral with an intentionally submitted complete contradictory model is a correct numeric outcome and an incorrect model outcome. Neither an incomplete model nor a rejected gesture establishes a model error.
- Require observable learner choices about the whole and grouping. Drag, tap and VoiceOver may produce equivalent evidence through a pinned semantic counter state or structured marking with the same rubric. A prefilled partition, automatic complement or announcement of the required quantity supplies mathematical help. Preserve free-form drawing and allow neutral structured marking. Unverified drawing or incomplete marking leaves model pending. A separately identified human rubric route would require approval. A tuple such as 8/3/5 alone proves neither disjointness nor learner authorship. U07 Check assesses the partition and its link to the whole. If an authored target assesses recombination itself, require a witnessed clear-and-recombine action or an equivalent observed explanation.
- Keep requested route, actual support, opportunity, occurrence, attempt ordinal, response/model provenance, result and assessment purpose distinct. Guided and assisted work is useful practice evidence. Diagnostics and readiness provide routing observations. Demo, replay, access failures, skips and time passing produce no scored opportunity. Preserve all receipts, including partial observations. Achievement, pending coverage, review status and recommendation remain separate in reporting.

## Planned initial assessment (F2)

Each slot specifies comparable demands, contrast requirements and any model rubric. The contingency may replace a missing facet according to the prespecified map after a miss, or supply a later-date success. Choose its coverage before offering it by the pinned rule, without selecting away an observed error. A facet plan that cannot fit four successes stays pending for authoring review. Use meaningful contrasts, including zero where required, and sample related variants within the same plan. Keep the burden within short sittings in the 10–15 minute lesson plan.

1. Count at most one eligible first-response result per slot, across all reserved replacement occurrences. A complete result contains a confirmed numeral and a resolved model outcome where required. Count a complete incorrect numeral or contradictory model as a miss. A pending model records numeric evidence but cannot fill a scored slot. Allow neutral completion of the same locked response without re-entering its numeral or seeing answer-bearing feedback. If it cannot be completed independently, keep the slot unresolved and use only a pre-authorized substitute or leave it pending. Preserve the partial observation separately. 2. Require distinct canonical relationships within the group. An eligible first response may be on a new relationship or on a familiar relationship that meets the elapsed-time and independence rules below. Both require a confirmed response and sufficient learner-produced model where required. For an interrupted original offer, preserve its identity and first-response state. Record familiar independent retrieval separately from novel transfer. Claim novel transfer only where the relationship has no applicable prior presentation, practice, disclosure or scored use and the task meets reviewed transfer demand.
3. Stop successfully as soon as at least four complete successes collectively cover the required facets and models, span at least two successful learning dates and satisfy any documented component prerequisite. Before offering the last planned opportunity, check whether a success on that slot, at the proposed learning date and with its planned demand, could meet the count, date and coverage requirements together. Reserve an unconsumed planned slot for a later learning date when a success then is necessary. If a required facet is missing, use only a candidate that can supply it under the pinned plan and reviewed substitutions. A calendar date with only a miss supplies no successful date. Never add an unplanned opportunity or exceed the five-slot cap under this proposal.
4. Evaluate completed evidence before closing. When it falls short, close as short if all planned slots are consumed or if no combination of successes on the remaining planned slots could meet the count, successful-date and facet requirements. Identify the missing evidence and preserve every receipt. An already consumed four-slot plan with four successes on one date closes short for missing successful-date coverage. A group with a reserved fifth that could still complete the coverage can wait for that opportunity on a later eligible date. Treat a pause, an unresolved model or access issue, clock uncertainty, or temporary candidate unavailability as pending only while completion remains possible under the pinned plan. Resume the same plan and apply the existing eligibility checks. A pause adds no score or retry cost. If the plan is short, offer instruction or a separately planned later group under the applicable eligibility rules. Incompatible work requires explicit supersession with provenance.
